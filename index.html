Creative work

Hot Air, a climate myth-busting podcast

Most people no longer deny climate change. They are buried under mixed messages, and that doubt turns into delay. Hot Air is my answer, a short podcast where each episode takes one climate myth, shows how it spread, and corrects it with evidence in a story busy listeners can finish on a walk to class or commute to work. I built the proposal on peer-reviewed research for listeners ages 18 to 30 who care about the climate but have no time to read article after article.
